Bonding milestone, Justin Krueger vesting at $18M valuation: LV charges each bonded project 1.50% in bonding revenue and pays the surety 0.74% as the actual bond cost (Tier 1). The 0.76% spread is real cash received by LV, and it funds Krueger's equity purchase at an $18M valuation. Bonding these four projects pushes Krueger across his 10% bonding vesting threshold.

FY 2026 dollar amount: bonding revenue of $1,405,538 (1.50% on the in-year bonded billing base of ~$93.7M, including the four pending closings) less surety cost of $693,399 (0.74%) leaves a ~$712K spread in FY 2026 alone. This $712K is already included in the $1.78M consolidated net income shown on Slide 01 of the SPC update, not an addition on top.
What changes after these four: The surety steps to Tier 2 at 3.25% on the next round of projects, which would compress the bonding spread materially against the same 1.50% project charge. The mitigant, and the path baked into the SPC Extended BD plan, is for LV to stand up its own bonding capacity, which removes the third-party surety cost line entirely (this is the assumption referenced in the SPC scenario plan slide for FY 2029+).
Why these four matter together: Closing all four wraps the LV expansion. The PMs, supers, and back-office capacity stand up against this book and don't need to grow further before the BD pipeline (Slide 05) starts feeding them new work. From here on, every dollar of new construction billing lands on a stable cost base.

Reporting Cadence Going Forward

Monthly · Skinny Deck

~30 minutes · KPI trending & exceptions

A concise monthly update for IC-context. Leads with the headline KPIs (revenue, margin, cash, bonding, backlog) trending against budget and prior period, plus exception reporting on any active job or pipeline item that moved meaningfully. Built as a tighter subset of the quarterly package, not a separate deliverable.

  • Headline financial KPIs vs. budget and prior period
  • Pipeline + backlog deltas since last month
  • Active job exceptions (schedule, buyout, change orders)
  • Forward-look on closings and starts in the next 60 days
Quarterly · Full Board Package

The deeper dive · this Q1 2026 deck is the model

The full quarterly artifact, scheduled after quarter close so KPIs reflect a complete three-month period rather than a single-month snapshot. Distributed as a pre-read in advance of the meeting with the appendix as supporting reference. The narrative front sections (Company Story, LV 2.0 direction) carry the strategic story; the back sections carry the financial detail.

  • Full P&L, balance sheet, cash flow forecast
  • WIP report and job-level KPIs across active projects
  • Risk register, safety scorecard, compliance status
  • Strategic direction, prior-quarter commitments, forward priorities

Outputs That Matter · What Steyn Will See Each Cycle

The four groups below are the curated subset that flows from the LV package up to the Six Peak / Steyn board view. Everything inside the Q1 deck supports these; the headline metrics here are what carry the conversation.

Group 01

Financial Health

YTD and LTM views, vs. budget, with variance commentary. Reconciled across the deck (one value per KPI, period-stamped).

  • Revenue, gross margin, overhead ratio, net margin (YTD + LTM)
  • LTM Adjusted EBITDA
  • Operating cash, working capital, balance sheet KPIs (current ratio, days of cash, ROE)
  • Quarterly cash-flow forecast plus break-even revenue commentary
Group 02

Capacity & Bonding

The capacity to absorb new work and the cost of standing it up, including the path to LV-direct bonding referenced in the SPC Extended BD plan.

  • Bonding capacity available and utilization (% of program consumed)
  • Cost of bonding (current Krueger spread vs. path to direct LV bonding)
  • Travelers conditional approval status on Francis and the next bonded job
  • Forward-look on direct-bonding pathway as Wilton and HVN jobs deliver
Group 03

Backlog & Pipeline

The forward visibility, with shared definitions so backlog and pipeline are not used interchangeably between LV and Steyn.

  • Total backlog (remaining contract value on awarded / contracted projects only)
  • Active bid pipeline value and pursuit count, with bid hit rate
  • Top client concentration (HVN share of backlog and pipeline)
  • Pipeline-to-backlog funnel: tracking, bidding, finalist, awarded, contracted
Group 04

Project Execution & Safety

The job-level health of the active book, plus the safety profile that underpins the bonded GC posture.

  • Per-project WIP: contract value, change orders, % billed, % complete, GP%, NP%
  • Buyout progress and savings per project (where the GMP risk lives)
  • Job-level risk register (low / medium / high) with schedule status per job
  • TRIR, DART, sub injury rates, near-miss tracking (LV 2.0 safety system)
How this rolls up to the SPC view Steyn already sees: The financial outputs above feed directly into the GC Revenue, GC Costs, and Bonding Outstanding lines on the SPC Section 01 KPI tiles, the consolidated cash positions on the Scenario Plan, and the FY 2026 / FY 2027 NI build that drives the Base Case versus Extended BD comparison. Establishing this as the standing reporting set means each future board cycle works off the same definitions and the same artifact, not a fresh assembly.
